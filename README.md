# Hi, I'm Adewale 👋

**Founder & CEO, [RavenLabs Solutions](https://ravenlabs.ng)** · Lagos, Nigeria 🇳🇬

I build products and infrastructure across mobile value-added services, fintech, and digital commerce, for markets from Nigeria to the UK and beyond: the subscription engines, payment rails, and fraud defences that most people never see but use every day.

I've spent the last several years shipping production systems in telco VAS and fintech, from carrier-integrated SMS/IVR services to wallet engines and the backend of a licensed bank. I'm still hands-on: most days you'll find me deep in backend services, database tuning, or observability dashboards.

---

### 🛠️ What I'm building

- **Quiver Ads** — performance marketing for VAS operators, now growing into analytics and fraud prevention (device fingerprinting, TLS/header signals, traffic-quality scoring).
- **PredictPro** — a skill-based football prediction and trivia platform, where fans compete on what they know about the game rather than on luck.
- **Tryve** — a multi-channel AI sales assistant for merchants, answering customers, taking orders and collecting payments across the channels they already sell on, all from one place.
- **Wiver** — a B2B wallet engine and banking-as-a-service core.
- **Mobile VAS services & games** — a portfolio of subscription services and mobile games, delivered over SMS, USSD, IVR and the web, and billed directly through mobile carriers.

### 💡 Areas I care about

`Telco VAS & DCB` · `Mobile identity & Network APIs (CAMARA / Open Gateway)` · `Fraud detection` · `Wallets & core banking` · `Conversational commerce`
